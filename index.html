Float Up — Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 22, 2026  ·  Last updated: April 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AIS GAMES ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use the mobile application Float Up (the "App"). By installing or using the App, you agree to the practices described below.

Data controller: AIS GAMES
Contact email: aisgamesemail@gmail.com

1. Scope

This policy applies to the Float Up mobile application distributed through the Google Play Store and Apple App Store. It does not apply to any third-party services, websites, or applications that may be linked from within the App.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information you provide

The App does not require account creation and does not ask you to provide personal information directly. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or payment details.

2.2 Information stored on your device

The App stores gameplay-related data locally on your device using the operating system's standard preferences API (Capacitor Preferences). This includes:

  • Level progress, scores, achievements, pearls collected
  • Game settings (sound, music, language, HUD scale)
  • Cooldown timestamps related to in-app reward displays

This data never leaves your device, is not transmitted to us, and is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its storage through your device settings.

2.3 Information collected automatically by third parties

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob automatically collects certain information for the purpose of serving and measuring ads:

  • Advertising identifier — Google Advertising ID (GAID) on Android, Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) on iOS
  • Device information — device model, operating system and version, language, screen size
  • Approximate location — derived from your IP address (country and city level; no precise GPS data is accessed)
  • Ad interaction data — which ads were shown, whether you watched a rewarded ad to completion, whether you clicked

3. How We Use Information

We use information for the following purposes:

  • To provide the App — saving your progress locally so you can resume gameplay
  • To show advertisements — serving relevant ads through Google AdMob and managing the frequency of ads shown to you
  • To offer optional rewarded advertisements — allowing you to watch an ad voluntarily in exchange for an in-game benefit (e.g., continuing after a failed attempt)
  • To improve the App — using aggregated, non-identifying metrics from AdMob to understand general usage patterns

4. Legal Basis (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, or Switzerland, our legal basis for processing personal data through advertising partners is:

  • Consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)) — for personalized advertising. You will be shown a consent request on first launch through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). You may accept, decline, or update your choice at any time.
  • Legitimate interests (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)) — for non-personalized advertising, fraud prevention, and basic analytics necessary to run the App.

5. Advertising and Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party service to monetize the App. You are encouraged to review their privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob (Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited) — privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy and AdMob-specific details at support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

AdMob may share data with its network of demand partners (advertisers and ad exchanges) to deliver ads. When you consent to personalized advertising, these partners may process your advertising identifier and device information to select relevant ads.

6. Your Choices and Controls

6.1 Reset or disable your advertising identifier

  • Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads → "Delete advertising ID" or "Reset advertising ID"
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → disable "Allow Apps to Request to Track", or Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising → disable "Personalized Ads"

6.2 Update your consent

You can change your advertising consent choices at any time. For EU/UK/Swiss users, the consent form will re-appear when required by regulation.

6.3 Uninstall the App

Uninstalling the App permanently removes all locally stored game data. No trace of your progress remains on our side because we do not operate servers that store user data.

7. Rights of EU / UK / Swiss Users (GDPR)

If you are located in the EEA, United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you have the following rights:

  • Right of access — request confirmation of whether we process your personal data
  • Right to rectification — correct inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten") — request deletion of your data
  • Right to restrict processing
  • Right to object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Right to data portability
  • Right to withdraw consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing
  • Right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at aisgamesemail@gmail.com. Because we do not collect direct identifiers, you may need to provide information that helps us identify the data you are asking about (such as an approximate date of install or your advertising identifier).

8. Rights of California Residents (CCPA / CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you have the following rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA:

  • Right to know what categories of personal information we collect and how it is used
  • Right to delete personal information we hold about you
  • Right to correct inaccurate personal information
  • Right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information. While we do not sell data in the traditional sense, the sharing of advertising identifiers with AdMob's partners for personalized advertising may be considered a "sale" or "sharing" under the CPRA. You may opt out through the in-app consent form or by contacting us.
  • Right to limit use of sensitive personal information
  • Right to non-discrimination for exercising these rights

9. Rights of Other U.S. State Residents

Residents of Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Utah, and other U.S. states with consumer privacy laws have similar rights, including the right to access, correct, delete, and opt out of targeted advertising. Contact us to exercise these rights.

10. Children's Privacy

Float Up is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us at aisgamesemail@gmail.com and we will promptly delete the relevant data. AdMob is configured with an age-appropriate content rating; only ads rated T (Teen) or below are shown.

11. International Data Transfers

Google AdMob processes data on servers located in the United States and other countries. Where required, Google relies on appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission to transfer personal data outside the EEA.

12. Data Retention

We do not retain personal data on any server. Game progress stored on your device remains until you delete it. Data processed by AdMob is retained according to Google's own retention policies — see Google's privacy documentation for details.

13. Data Security

Game data stored locally on your device is protected by the security mechanisms of your operating system (sandboxing, per-app storage isolation). Data sent to AdMob is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS). However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, or legal requirements. The updated version will be posted at the same URL with a new "Last updated" date. Material changes will be announced within the App where practicable. Your continued use of the App after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
